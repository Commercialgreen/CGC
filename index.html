About us

Cleaning that doesn't cost you the air in the room.

Commercial Green Cleaning was founded on a straightforward idea: a building can be spotless without filling it with fumes. Conventional janitorial products leave behind residues and volatile compounds that linger in carpet, upholstery, and recirculated air long after the crew has gone home.

We use Green Seal and EPA Safer Choice certified products across every account, paired with HEPA filtration and microfiber systems that capture particulates instead of pushing them around. Our crews are background checked, uniformed, and trained on your building’s specific protocols before their first shift.

FAQ

Common questions

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is based on square footage, the number of restrooms and break rooms, floor types, and how often we visit. We provide a fixed monthly rate after a free on-site walkthrough — not an hourly estimate that drifts upward.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers’ compensation coverage, and every employee is bonded and background checked. We’re happy to provide a certificate of insurance naming your company as an additional insured.

What does “green cleaning” actually mean?

It means every product we use carries a Green Seal or EPA Safer Choice certification, and that we pair those products with HEPA-filtered vacuums and microfiber systems. Certification matters — “natural” and “eco-friendly” are marketing terms with no legal definition behind them.

Can you clean outside of our business hours?

Most of our accounts are serviced between 6 PM and 5 AM. We can also schedule early mornings, weekends, or daytime porter service if your space needs attention while occupied.

Do we need to supply anything?

No. We bring all equipment and cleaning products. We can also manage restocking of paper goods, soap, and liners and bill them at cost if you’d like to consolidate that line item.

How long is the contract?

Our standard agreement runs twelve months with a 30-day cancellation clause for either party. We don’t use auto-renewing multi-year contracts.

What happens if something is missed?

Call or email us and we’ll return within one business day to correct it at no charge. Every visit is logged against a checklist you approve up front, so there’s a clear record of what was and wasn’t completed.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the greater metro area and surrounding counties. If you’re unsure whether your building is in range, call us — if we can’t take it on, we’ll usually know someone who can.
