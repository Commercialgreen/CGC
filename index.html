What We Offer

Whether your needs are simple or complex, we offer a range of comprehensive services that can be customized.

Recurring Facility Care 8 services

Janitorial Service

Our most popular service. We offer daily, weekly, or customized cleaning schedules tailored to your facility’s needs. We handle everything from routine tidying to deep cleaning across multiple types of facilities.

Day Porter Service

Our dedicated day porters provide real-time facility management during business hours. We respond to spills, messes, and maintenance requests immediately to keep your space guest-ready throughout the day while your team stays productive.

Lobby & Common Areas

Lobby and common spaces are where first impressions form. Whether you manage an HOA, multi-tenant building, hospital or any high-traffic facility, our team can be routinely scheduled to create a welcoming environment that reflects your company’s standards. We keep lobbies, reception areas, and common spaces immaculate.

Restroom Restocking & Cleaning

We love clean, well-stocked and organized restrooms. We manage everything from daily sanitizing to restocking soap, towels, and other essentials, ensuring your facilities meet health and cleanliness standards.

Supplies & Consumables Restocking

We keep your essential supplies stocked. From cleaning products, paper goods, soap, and other consumables, we proactively manage inventory so you never face shortages and your operations run smoothly.

Trash & Recycling Management

We handle regular trash removal, recycling coordination, and can customize collection schedules to match your business needs. Let us ensure efficient waste management that keeps your facility organized and environmentally responsible.

Surface & Touch Sanitization

High-touch surfaces spread germs. We regularly clean and disinfect door handles, light switches, countertops, railings, and more in order to maintain a hygienic workspace for your team and visitors.

Security & Facility Oversight

We provide an extra set of eyes on your facility to provide peace of mind. Our porters monitor for maintenance issues, security concerns, and unusual activity while keeping your space clean.

Specialized & Surface Care 7 services

Carpet Cleaning

Professional carpet cleaning extends the life of your flooring and removes deep-set dirt, allergens, and stains. We use advanced equipment and eco-friendly cleaning solutions to refresh your carpets and improve indoor air quality.

Floor Care & Maintenance

From hardwood and concrete to tile and vinyl, we provide specialized care for every flooring type. Our floor care services keep surfaces looking new and extend floor lifespan through proper cleaning and protective treatments.

Marble & Granite Surface Care

Natural stone requires specialized treatment to maintain its beauty and integrity. We provide expert marble and granite cleaning, polishing, and sealing services for countertops and floors to protect and keep premium surfaces radiant.

Window Cleaning (Interior & Exterior + Doors)

Crystal-clear windows transform how your facility looks and feels. Our professional window cleaning services cover interior and exterior glass plus doors, ensuring optimal visibility and a bright, welcoming appearance throughout your building.

Upholstery Cleaning

Office furniture, lobby seating, and conference room upholstery accumulate dust, stains, and odors. Professional upholstery cleaning restores fabric, removes allergens, and extends the life of your furnishings.

Leather Cleaning & Conditioning

Leather furniture makes a sophisticated statement and requires proper maintenance. We provide specialized leather cleaning and conditioning to keep premium seating looking executive-quality.

Computer Room Cleaning

Tech environments demand pristine conditions. We provide specialized cleaning for computer rooms using static-free equipment and protocols that protect sensitive electronics while eliminating dust and contaminants.

Projects & One-Time 4 services

Special Events Cleaning

Preparing for a major event or cleaning up after one? We coordinate with your timeline to prepare spaces before events or provide comprehensive post-event cleanup.

Power Washing & Pressure Washing

High-powered water cleaning removes years of accumulated dirt, grime, mold, oil and mildew from exterior surfaces from building facades to sidewalks and parking lots. We adjust pressure levels and techniques for different materials to ensure effective cleaning without damage.

Construction Cleanup

During construction phases, we provide ongoing cleanup to manage dust, debris, and disruption. Regular cleanups minimize impact on project areas, new or old, and keep your clients happy.

Post-Construction Deep Clean

After your contractors finish, our team delivers complete facility restoration. We remove dust from every surface, clean windows, floors, and fixtures, dispose of construction debris, and ensure your facility is move-in ready.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is included in standard commercial cleaning?

Our standard cleaning package includes sweeping, vacuuming, mopping, dusting, disinfecting high-touch areas like door handles and light switches, breakroom and kitchen cleaning, trash removal, surface wiping, restocking cleaning inventory, and sanitizing and restocking bathrooms.

Do you offer after-hours or business-hours cleaning?

We offer both — let us know whichever works best for your business. We also provide day porter services for continuous maintenance in high-traffic areas throughout the workday.

Are your cleaning products safe for employees with allergies?

Yes. We use eco-friendly cleaning products and HEPA-filter vacuums to minimize allergens. If your team has specific allergies or sensitivities, we can discuss alternative products and protocols.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment?

No. We provide all commercial-grade equipment and eco-friendly cleaning solutions, so you don’t need to supply anything. If you’d prefer we use specific products or equipment of your own, we’re glad to work with what you provide.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. We offer flexible month-to-month agreements that adapt to your business needs.

Do you offer specialized or deep cleaning services?

Yes. We provide deep sanitization, post-construction cleanup, carpet steam cleaning and extraction, and interior window washing.

Do I need to be present during cleaning?

No. Most of our clients provide us with a key, security code, or lockbox access so we can clean before business hours or after your team leaves for the day.

Are your cleaners insured and bonded?

Yes. Our team is fully insured and bonded for your protection and peace of mind.

How do I get a quote?

Contact us by phone or email to request a free quote. We’ll schedule a brief walkthrough of your facility to take measurements and understand your specific cleaning needs before providing a formal proposal.

How do you handle keys and security codes?

We treat security access with the highest priority. Keys and codes are stored securely and handled only by our trusted team members. We’re also happy to accommodate any specific security protocols or access requirements your business has.
